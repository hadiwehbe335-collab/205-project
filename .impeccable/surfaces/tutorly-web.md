# Tutorly web experience

## Scope and mode

A minimal intro (Persuade) leading into a front-end-only university learning application (Operate), built specifically for a short live presentation.

## Audience, job, action

University students need to know what to study now, learn it from their own course material, check their understanding, practise it, and leave with a clear next step. The primary action is “Continue Study Session”; the path must make course organization, priorities, source-grounded teaching, practice, and personalization clear in two to three minutes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Tutorly is a guided learning loop, not a feature gallery or generic AI chat. The dashboard names one next step; the study session teaches one task at a time and always returns the student to a clear recommendation.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve the established cool white canvas, soft lilac fields, deep ink, confident indigo accent, mint provenance states, Manrope typography, fine blue-grey borders, and ambient depth. A compact ink sidebar anchors the app; the study session recedes to a quieter, narrower reading surface.

STORY: Hadi opens Database Systems, sees Second Normal Form as the recommended revision priority, confirms that five course files are ready, then completes Understand → Example → Check → Practice → Complete. Tutorly cites the relevant lecture and tutorial, updates progress, and recommends Functional Dependencies next.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The intro is a sparse centered entry screen with the product promise and one “Open Tutorly” action. The app opens to a compact sidebar and a generous dashboard whose dominant panel is “Continue Studying: Second Normal Form”; recommendations and recent material remain secondary.

FORM: Guided course workspace and focused lesson sequence, pinned by the requested Course materials → priorities → study session → next recommendation flow; seed: none (pinned) — “the prototype should feel like an actual learning experience” with a compact sidebar and low-information-density study stages.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

All content and interactions are local simulation. No live AI or RAG claims, no guaranteed assessment predictions, no grades promises, no diagnosis, and no assessed-work completion. Desktop and mobile layouts, keyboard focus, reduced motion, and semantic controls are required.
